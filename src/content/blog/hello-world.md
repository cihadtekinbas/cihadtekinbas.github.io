Welcome to my new home on the internet! 🎉

I built this site with [Astro](https://astro.build), and it's hosted for free on
[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com). It's a place for my **portfolio** and
my **blog**.

## What I'll write about

- Things I'm learning
- Projects I'm working on
- Notes and tutorials that might help others

## How to add a post

Posts are just Markdown files in `src/content/blog/`. Adding a new one looks
something like this:

```md
---
title: "My next post"
description: "A short summary."
pubDate: 2026-09-01
tags: ["notes"]
---

Content here in Markdown. It gets published automatically when I push.
```

Looking forward to sharing more. Stay tuned!
